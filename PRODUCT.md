# Product

## Register
brand

## Users & Purpose
JeenLabs is a software studio site for founders and product teams evaluating automation, web, and custom software partners. Visitors arrive to judge craft quickly, understand services, and start a conversation.

Primary job: build trust through the work posture (dark cinematic studio), then convert via contact (`contact@jeenlabs.com`).

## Brand Personality
Cinematic · Precise · Partner-like

## Anti-references
Generic AI purple SaaS landings, cream editorial templates, Inter-only startup kits, fake social-proof walls, three equal feature cards with stock icons as the whole story.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG AA. Forced dark theme. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Visible focus rings on all interactive controls. Contact remains mailto with a visible email fallback.

## Design principles
1. Keep forced dark, brand red `#dc2626`, Inter + Geist Mono + Jost.
2. One job per section; hero stays brand + headline + one CTA + canvas.
3. Prefer spacing and type hierarchy over card chrome.
4. Motion supports hierarchy (GSAP / Three), never noise.
5. Real copy over agency filler; testimonials/work stay off until content is real.
